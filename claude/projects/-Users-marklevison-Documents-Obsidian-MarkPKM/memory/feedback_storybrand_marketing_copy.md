When drafting marketing/promo copy for Mark (course promos, social posts, sales pages), follow the StoryBrand frame and keep the tone measured.

**Why:** On a 2026-06 pass of "Surviving the AI Tsunami" course posts, Mark said the rhetoric was "a bit too strong" across the board and asked for StoryBrand: the reader/learner is the hero of their own story, Mark is "the guide on the side," and his own mistakes should be emphasized. Punchy/provocative lines ("watch motivation collapse," "first in line for replacement") read as too hard-sell for him.

**How to apply:**
- Hero = the reader (the ScrumMaster / Product Owner / coach). Lead with *their* problem and stakes, address them as "you," and make success theirs.
- Guide = Mark: empathy first ("I've made these mistakes too" — the receipt-images exposure, the 3-hour rabbit holes) plus quiet authority, never the centre of the story. Avoid Mark-CV-heavy openings.
- Give a plan and a gentle call to action ("have a look," "see if it fits"), not high-pressure scarcity by default — though beta price/seat-cap details can be added back when urgency is wanted.
- Dial the rhetoric down: calm, honest, evidence-led. Pairs with the [[reference-glass-one-third-full]] stance and [[feedback_persona_tone]] (avoid hype words).
