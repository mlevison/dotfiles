When proposing bullet formatting in Mark's copy reviews, use the pattern `**Bold Head** Capital sentence.` with no period after the bold head.

**Why:** Mark reads the bold portion as a heading, and headings don't take terminal punctuation. The period-after-bold pattern (Stripe-docs-style "inline label") is technically valid but conflicts with his instinct. Consistency across his writing is the goal; the no-period pattern matches it.

**How to apply:** Apply across all copy-review work ([[feedback-no-ai-edits-to-copy]] skills: copy-editing, apr-blog-critique, apr-newsletter-reviewer, apr-linkedin-style). When mixed bullet styles appear in a draft, recommend collapsing to `**Head** Capital sentence.` (no period after head). Don't propose the period variant even if it's stylistically defensible.
