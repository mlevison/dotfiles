Occasional bare "AI" is fine in Mark's writing, especially when describing public perception or how others talk (e.g. the LinkedIn/Mastodon "AI will replace developers" discourse). Don't flag those as needing "GenAI" or "LLM".

**Why:** The apr-newsletter-reviewer rubric says use "GenAI"/"LLM" for the generative tools and reserve bare "AI" for broad discussion. Reporting the public conversation is its own legitimate context, alongside "discussing AI broadly" - the crowd says "AI", so quoting/paraphrasing them in those words is accurate, not a slip.

**How to apply:** When reviewing, only flag bare "AI" where Mark is making his own claim about what the generative tools actually do/can't do. Leave it where he's characterising others' beliefs, public hype, or the general discourse. When unsure, lean toward leaving it. See the GenAI/LLM rule in `Skills/apr-newsletter-reviewer.md`.
