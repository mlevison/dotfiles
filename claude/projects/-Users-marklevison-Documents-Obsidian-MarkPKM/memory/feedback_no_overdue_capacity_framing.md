Never frame suggestions with pressure or deadline language like "overdue," "you should," "you need to." APR is a one-person business and capacity isn't infinite. Present findings as options Mark can pick from based on his capacity, not obligations.

**Why:** Manufactured urgency (e.g. turning "12 days since last newsletter" into an "overdue task") adds stress without adding value, and doesn't respect that he's the only person doing the work.

**How to apply:** In analytics briefs, weekly reviews, and any recap, offer observations and options ("this theme is warm if you want to build on it," "note for whenever the next issue goes out"). Don't invent tasks from gaps in cadence. Don't rank things as behind schedule. Related: [[feedback_weekly_analytics_run_scripts]], [[feedback_no_unsolicited_files]].
