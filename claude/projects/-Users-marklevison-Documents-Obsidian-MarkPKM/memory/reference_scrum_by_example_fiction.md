Blog posts in Agile Pain Relief's **Scrum by Example** series are fictionalized teaching stories, not accounts of things Mark personally saw. The recurring cast (the World's Smallest Online Bookstore team; characters like Steve the ScrumMaster, Paula the Product Owner, Ian, Jan) is invented to illustrate Scrum dynamics.

When summarizing or re-cutting one (social drafts, blog critique, newsletter), present it **as a story** ("here's how one goes sideways", present tense) or attribute it ("a Scrum-by-Example teaching story"). Never write "I watched this happen", "recently I saw", or "in a real Sprint Review" over fictional material, it manufactures a false first-hand claim. First-person "I saw" is only accurate when the source is Mark's own real experience (e.g. a newsletter recounting something he actually did).
