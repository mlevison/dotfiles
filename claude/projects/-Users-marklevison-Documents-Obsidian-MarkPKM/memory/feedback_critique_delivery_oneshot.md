Mark's default (from CLAUDE.md) is to deliver critiques/reviews section by section, pausing between. But when he explicitly says "one shot," "all at once," or similar, deliver the entire critique in a single response instead.

**Why:** For a final/last-pass review he already knows the piece and wants the whole punch-list at once, not a paced walkthrough.

**How to apply:** Honor an explicit "one shot" / "do it all at once" request over the section-by-section default. Absent that signal, keep the default paced delivery.
