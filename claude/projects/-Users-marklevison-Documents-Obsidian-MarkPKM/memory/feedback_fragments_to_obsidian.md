When triaging the Drafts inbox, items Mark labels as "fragments" (short free-writing pieces, taglines, book-inspired snippets) should be proposed for **Obsidian (the vault)**, not appended to `APR/Efforts/Content Ideas.md`. Mark distinguishes fragments (developing prose/ideas he wants to keep and grow in Obsidian) from one-line content hooks (which go to Content Ideas).

**Do NOT propose `APR/Efforts/Free Writing/`** — as of 2026-07-26 Mark confirmed that folder is **read-only, kept for historical purposes** (see [[reference_free_writing_read_only]]). It is no longer a valid write target for new fragments.

**Why:** During the 2026-07-19 Drafts triage he overrode a proposal to append a Reshuffle-book fragment to Content Ideas, saying "Fragments - always suggest sending them Obsidian." A week later he flagged that Free Writing (my previous default destination) is frozen.

**How to apply:** During [[drafts-inbox]] triage, classify fragment-type items as vault-bound, but **ask Mark for the destination** rather than defaulting to Free Writing. In practice he has often chosen to just leave content/fragment items in the Drafts inbox as active.
