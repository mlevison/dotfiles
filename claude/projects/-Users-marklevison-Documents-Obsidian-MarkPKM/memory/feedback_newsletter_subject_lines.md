For APR newsletter subject lines, Mark's style preferences:

- **Avoid obscure insider jargon.** "5hr compute blocks" / "compute" is too obscure for a subject line, even though it appears in the body. Subject lines must make sense to a subscriber who hasn't read the issue yet.
- **Never use "I have the receipts"** (or "I got a receipt") as a phrasing. He dislikes the idiom.
- **What works:** the "big number, deflating payoff" hook, e.g. "150 agents, two useful insights." A large concrete number paired with a small return; the irony carries it. No hype words.

**Why:** Keeps subject lines readable to cold subscribers and in his voice.

**How to apply:** When suggesting subject lines, lead with number-plus-deflation options and plain theme lines; skip jargon-dependent or idiom-heavy phrasings. Relates to [[feedback_persona_tone]] and [[reference_glass_one_third_full]].
