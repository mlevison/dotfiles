Italicised passages in Mark's draft sales pages, course outlines, and similar working documents are author hints to himself. They will not appear in the published version. Do not flag them as "editor's notes to remove" or "scaffolding visible to buyers."

**Why:** Mark uses italics as a working-draft convention. Treating those lines as buyer-facing copy is a misread and creates noise in the critique.

**How to apply:** When critiquing any APR draft (sales pages, course outlines, blog drafts, etc.), treat `*italicised passages*` as out-of-scope author scaffolding. Comment on their substance only if Mark explicitly asks. Focus the critique on the non-italic body text.
