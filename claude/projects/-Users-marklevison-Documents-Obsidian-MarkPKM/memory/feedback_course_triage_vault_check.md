When triaging or ranking Readwise documents for relevance to an APR course outline, do NOT rely only on the outline file's explicit citation list to filter out already-known sources. Mark keeps companion notes per source elsewhere in the vault, and many course-relevant articles he's already read and cited are referenced under different titles or filenames than the Readwise document title.

**Why:** On the Pragmatic AI For Agile Teams course triage (2026-05-13), I matched only against the outline file at `APR/Efforts/EducationalContent/Pragmatic AI For Agile Teams - Course Outline.md`. Five of the ten picks (DevAI, GitClear AI Copilot Code Quality, Army of Juniors, Human-Written vs AI-Generated Code, AI Assistance Reduces Persistence) were already read and cited by Mark in his vault notes, so the picks wasted his time.

**How to apply:**
- Before presenting course-relevance picks, grep the vault (especially `APR/` and `Notes/`) for each candidate's title fragments AND author name.
- Treat any vault hit as evidence Mark has already mined the source, and either drop the pick or label it clearly as "already in vault."
- Show the user the cross-check method up-front so the misses are surfaced before they invest in reviewing the list.
- The outline file is one signal among many; vault grep is the broader signal.
