In Mark's writing (and when helping him help others reframe), use "missed opportunity" in place of "failure," and frame setbacks as learning moments rather than things done wrong.

**Why:** Drawn from Marcus Buckingham's *Design Love In* (Bookworm ep. 250, the "Major Missed Opportunities" / MMO tool). A missed opportunity is "not things we're doing wrong, it's just things we could do better" - it strips out the blame and negativity and keeps the framing forward-looking and improvement-focused, which fits Mark's evidence-based, glass-one-third-full voice. See [[reference_glass_one_third_full]].

**How to apply:** When reviewing or suggesting edits to Mark's writing, flag bare "failure" / "we did this wrong" framing and offer "missed opportunity" or a learning-moment reframe instead. It's a suggestion, not a hard rule - a genuine failure can stay a failure when that's the honest word. Deliver via inline CriticMarkup like other voice corrections (see [[feedback_criticmarkup_terse_corrections]]).
