When drafting LinkedIn / Mastodon / Bluesky copy that links to `https://agilepainrelief.com/quiz/ai-failure-modes/`, the CTA must:

1. **Name the subject explicitly** — use the phrase "AI Failure Modes in Development Teams" (or close variant). Vague CTAs like "test your knowledge of how AI fails" don't tell the reader what they're being invited to.
2. **Frame as a knowledge test, not a learning resource** — challenge the reader's understanding, don't promise to teach them.

Vary the wording across posts so a reader scrolling several in a week doesn't feel like they're reading the same post. Working variants:

- "Test your knowledge of AI Failure Modes in Development Teams."
- "How well do you know the AI Failure Modes hitting Development Teams? Test yourself."
- "Think you can spot the AI Failure Modes in Development Teams? Test yourself."
- "Think you know the AI Failure Modes in Development Teams? Test yourself."
- "How many AI Failure Modes in Development Teams can you actually identify? Test yourself."
- "How many AI Failure Modes can you spot in Development Teams?"

Avoid:
- "Twelve questions on how GenAI fails. Three minutes." (sounds like a lesson; subject is vague)
- "Test your knowledge of how AI fails." (test framing is there, but subject is vague — the reader doesn't know it's about Development Teams)
- "Learn about how AI is failing." (passive consumption)
- "See what the research says about AI failures." (article framing)

**Why:** Mark posted Day 1 on 2026-05-29 and noticed the quiz-linked posts weren't clearly inviting people to take a test about a specific subject. The earlier framing ("test your knowledge of how AI fails") had the challenge tone right but was too generic. The landing-page subject is specifically "AI Failure Modes in Development Teams" — the social copy needs to match. Without that specificity the reader doesn't know what the test is about, so the click rate suffers.

**How to apply:** Keep the data hook in the body (METR 19% slower, 1.57x security defects, etc.) — those still lead the post. The closing CTA names "AI Failure Modes in Development Teams" and frames it as a test. Include the "twelve questions, three minutes" descriptor on LinkedIn / longer posts, but it sits behind the subject + challenge, not in front of it.
