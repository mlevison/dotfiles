 .  7 ## 2026-08-06 — The modal popup is gone, replaced by a sticky bar and a slide-in
 .  8 
 .  9 **Why.** The modal darkened the page, locked scrolling, and trapped focus 30
 . 10 seconds into every blog post and glossary entry. That pattern is the one Google
 . 11 penalises as a mobile interstitial, and it reads as a third-party ad rather than
 . 12 as Mark's own voice. Research into what actually drives quiz starts pointed at
 . 13 two non-blocking surfaces instead: a **sticky bar** for browsing pages and a
 . 14 **slide-in** for articles, where the reader is already engaged.
 . 15 
 . 16 **One pinned surface per page, held by the routes.** A page shows the sticky bar
 . 17 or the slide-in, never both, because no route imports both components. There is
 . 18 no shared layout flag to keep in sync and no invariant to remember. The in-flow
 . 19 surfaces (CTA banner, link sidebar) are untouched by the rule: the hazards it
 . 20 guards against are physical ones about pinned elements stacking at an edge.
 . 21 
 . 22 **Where each one goes.** Slide-in: blog posts and glossary entries, exactly where
 . 23 the modal used to be. Sticky bar: home, the blog and glossary index pages,
 . 24 blog/all, blog categories, and content pages by default. Content pages are
 . 25 excluded when they're `noIndex` (all of those are mid-flow for something else)
 . 26 plus `privacy-policy` and `contact-us` by name. That last part is deliberately
 . 27 _not_ derived from `noIndex` alone: 404 is `noIndex` and got excluded on its own
 . 28 merits, `privacy-policy` isn't `noIndex` at all, and "don't index this" and
 . 29 "don't advertise on this" are two intentions that merely overlap.
 . 30 
 . 31 **Different suppression for different asks.** The slide-in is suppressed 15 days
 . 32 in `localStorage`, timed from when it appears rather than when it is dismissed —
 . 33 the same clock the modal used, so the launch numbers stay comparable and only the
 . 34 surface changed. The sticky bar is dismissable for the rest of the session only,
 . 35 in `sessionStorage`; it's on nearly every page, so a fortnight of silence would
 . 36 be too much, and `sessionStorage` means "session" needs no date arithmetic. The
 . 37 slide-in got a fresh storage key rather than inheriting the modal's, so nobody
 . 38 starts out suppressed and the first fortnight's reach isn't understated.
 . 39 
 . 40 **No surface asks for an email.** All of them link to the quiz cover; the quiz's
 . 41 own capture step, after the last question, remains the only ask. Two things the
 . 42 research recommended were deliberately not done. **Prefill** — putting the quiz's
 . 43 first question in the slide-in and carrying the answer through — was designed and
 . 44 then shelved: it needs a new contract from the surface into `QuizStepper`, which
 . 45 has to be built as a general engine feature since the engine is meant to stay
 . 46 extractable, and it can't be attributed separately if it ships alongside the
 . 47 surface swap. What survives of the idea is cosmetic and free: the slide-in poses
 . 48 a question with two answer buttons that both go to the same place and differ only
 . 49 by `utm_content`, so the numbers still say which framing pulled. **The mid-quiz
 . 50 email gate and a 5–7 question count** were also declined — the repo built the
 . 51 `capturePosition: 'start'` gate, ran it, and reverted it, and the live quiz's
 . 52 eight questions are a settled decision, not drift.
 . 53 
 . 54 **Measurement.** `cta_shown` and `cta_dismissed` go to PostHog with
 . 55 `{ surface, attribution, path }`; clicks stay on autocapture via the existing
 . 56 `data-ph-capture-attribute-*` mechanism. `cta_shown` fires for the sticky bar too,
 . 57 even though its impressions are nearly pageviews — "nearly" being the problem,
 . 58 since three separate rules withhold it, and replaying those against history later
 . 59 is work nobody would do. `utm_medium` gained `sticky-bar` and `slide-in` as a
 . 60 typed union, because a typo there produces traffic that looks real but belongs to
 . 61 no surface and nothing else in the build would catch it.
 . 62 
 . 63 **Not done:** no automated tests (the repo has no unit-test framework and adding
 . 64 one was judged out of proportion to three lines of date arithmetic), and the
 . 65 sticky bar overlays content rather than reserving space for itself. The second is
 . 66 provisional — it permanently covers the last ~56px of a page until dismissed, and
 . 67 the `padding-bottom` fix is one CSS variable away if it looks wrong in use.
